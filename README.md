# Super Store Sales Analysis Project

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze Super Store sales performance and identify business trends that improve profitability, customer engagement, and operational efficiency. The analysis focuses on understanding sales distribution, profit contribution, customer segments, regional performance, product categories, and payment behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

## Questions / KPI’s

### Sales Performance KPI’s
- What is the total sales generated by the business?
- What is the total profit earned?
- Which region contributes the highest sales and profit?
- Which state generates maximum revenue?
- What are the monthly and yearly sales trends?
  
### Product Performance KPI’s
- Which product category performs best in terms of sales?
- Which sub-category generates the highest profit?
- Which products are loss-making?
- Which categories need improvement strategies?
  
### Customer Analysis KPI’s
- Which customer segment contributes the highest sales?
- How many unique customers are present?
- Which customer segments are most profitable?

### Operational KPI’s
- Which shipping mode is most frequently used?
- What is the return percentage?
- Which payment mode is preferred by customers?

## Final Conclusion
The Super Store Sales Analysis project successfully identified key business drivers affecting sales and profitability. The business performs strongly in the West and East regions, while certain states like Texas and Pennsylvania require profitability improvement strategies.

Technology products contribute strong profits, whereas Furniture products need pricing or cost optimization due to lower margins. Consumer customers remain the primary revenue source, and COD is the most commonly used payment method.
